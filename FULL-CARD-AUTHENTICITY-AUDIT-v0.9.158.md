# v0.9.158 Full Card Authenticity Audit

- 915 runtime cards audited.
- 83 non-Move pages had conditional Momentum thresholds
  removed from play legality.
- Method colours are now limited to Move, Submission, Trademark and Momentum pages.
- Specials, Gameplans and Entrances use their original yellow page family.
- True printed Momentum requirements appear on card fronts and backs.
- Original rarity stars appear on both card faces.
- Cards without recovered rarity metadata remain unstarred rather than receiving
  an invented rarity.

Specific corrections:
- Irish Whip is a yellow Special and its “if you have 3 Strength” clause is an effect,
  not a play requirement.
- First Edition Power Bomb retains its printed 3 Strength play requirement.
