# v0.9.157 CPU Autocounter Strategy

The CPU now evaluates the exact post-payment hand before using an Autocounter.

It prefers payment combinations that preserve:

- an immediately affordable Move Page;
- a useful Momentum-plus-Move development path;
- Finishers, Submissions, counters and valuable Specials;
- enough retained card value to make the gained control useful.

It declines low-value Autocounters that would empty the hand or leave no playable follow-up. Emergency Autocounters remain available against lethal damage, dangerous Finishers at low HP, and submissions at critical HP.
