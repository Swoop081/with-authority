# Original Card Effects Certification — Phase 4

## Exact Momentum-page corrections

The following pages now use their original complete Momentum changes:

- Aggressive Agility: +1 Agility and +1 Attitude.
- Aggressive Strength: +1 Strength and +1 Attitude.
- Aggressive Technical: +1 Technical and +1 Attitude.
- Great Power: +1 Strength, +1 Strike, and -2 Attitude.
- Great Balance: +1 Agility, +1 Technical, and -2 Attitude.
- Great Leverage: +1 Strength, +1 Technical, and -2 Attitude.
- Great Strategy: +1 Knowledge, +1 Technical, and -2 Attitude.
- Great Impact: +1 Agility, +1 Strike, and -2 Attitude.

These changes are permanent method Momentum changes except for the explicit Attitude loss.

## Exact Move-connected Momentum effects

The original scripts now control the listed opponent Momentum losses:

- Snap Mare EX3: lose 1 Agility only when the opponent has at least 2.
- Elbow Drop EX3: lose 1 Strike only when the opponent has at least 2.
- Vertical Suplex EX3: lose 1 Technical only when the opponent has at least 2.
- Elbow to Back of the Head EX3: lose 1 Knowledge only when the opponent has at least 2.
- Neckbreaker EX3: lose 1 Strength only when the opponent has at least 2.
- Big Splash: lose 1 Technical.
- Short Arm Clothesline: put the opponent on the mat and lose 1 Knowledge when available.
- Stiff Forearm Shot: lose 1 Strength when available.

## High Cross Body

When played while the opponent is on the mat, the attacker draws exactly one page before
the move's counter window. The still-blocked automatic pin attempt remains outside this phase.

## No generic inference

These effects are keyed to exact original `sourceFile` values and are not inferred from
description wording.
