# v0.9.163 Continued Submission Draw

When the attacker ditches a page to maintain a submission:

1. submission damage resolves;
2. the next defender hold turn begins;
3. the defender draws one page;
4. the defender then chooses a Counter, Special or Autocounter.

The original draw that occurs when the submission first connects remains unchanged.
The additional draw occurs once for every later maintained hold turn.
