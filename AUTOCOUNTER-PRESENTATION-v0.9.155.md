# v0.9.155 Autocounter Presentation

A successful Autocounter no longer advances immediately through the control change,
draw and next CPU action.

The countered Move remains visible in the play pile with an AUTOCOUNTERED result.
A mandatory result panel identifies:

- the Move that was stopped;
- the wrestler who Autocountered;
- every discarded page;
- the exact Autocounter point total;
- who will gain control.

The match resumes only after CONTINUE is tapped. The next turn and its draw occur
after that acknowledgement, so the Autocounter cannot be missed outside the match log.
