# v0.9.165 Pin Rule Revert

The broad post-Move pin window introduced in v0.9.164 has been removed.

Pin attempts are once again available only when:
- both wrestlers are in the ring;
- the player has control;
- no submission is active;
- the opponent is Grounded/on the mat;
- the Attitude pin cost can be paid;
- no referee or original-script restriction blocks the attempt.

Lou Thesz Press is left unchanged until its authentic resulting position is confirmed.
